A PRIVATE PLACE

If You
Ever Wondered

Some things are easier to write
than to say.

This was never meant to be found.

01 / THE DAY WE MET

It started
with a screen.

No dramatic beginning. No movie moment. Just two people who happened to find each other online.

Somehow, the conversations kept getting longer. The jokes got easier. The silences stopped feeling awkward.

Neither of us announced that something had changed. There wasn't a moment where either of us said, "This is becoming something."

It just quietly became easier to talk to you than it was to explain why I wanted to.

And somehow, you became familiar without ever being beside me.

02 / THINGS I NEVER SAID

Maybe you noticed.
Maybe you didn't.

Sometimes I flirted because I wanted to.

Sometimes I said things that were probably a little more than friendly.

Then I'd act like they meant nothing.

Maybe you did the same. Maybe you didn't.

I never really asked.

Sometimes I wondered what would happen if one of us ever stopped pretending those little moments were just little moments.

But wondering is easy. Asking is different.

So we stayed somewhere between "just talking" and something neither of us named.

03 / WHY I PUSHED YOU AWAY

This is the part
I couldn't explain.

There were things I wanted to tell you.

And there were things I knew I couldn't.

Not because you did anything wrong. Not because I didn't trust you.

Some truths change the way people look at you once they're spoken.

And I wasn't ready to find out whether you'd look at me differently.

So whenever things started feeling a little too real...

I stepped back.

You might have wondered why. You might have thought I was losing interest. Maybe you thought I was playing games.

The truth is, I was trying to protect something.

I just never figured out whether I was protecting you, or myself.

04 / THE THINGS YOU GAVE ME

You probably
don't know.

You gave me ordinary things.

Conversations. Random jokes. Teasing. Late replies. Those completely unnecessary arguments that somehow became fun.

Nothing that would look important if someone else read it.

But ordinary things become important when you aren't expecting them to.

You made certain days lighter.

You gave me reasons to check my phone a little more often than I should have.

And sometimes, without knowing it, you made a bad day feel less bad.

Funny how someone can become part of your days without ever knowing they did.

05 / IF THINGS WERE DIFFERENT

I sometimes
wonder.

Not about some perfect future.

Just small things.

Sometimes I wonder what it would be like to hear your voice without a screen between us.

Would we be awkward for the first few minutes?

Would we laugh about it?

Would everything feel exactly the same... or completely different?

I don't let myself imagine much beyond that.

Because once you start imagining a future, it's easy to forget that you never promised one.

Maybe we meet someday.

Maybe we never do.

Maybe life takes us somewhere neither of us expects.

I don't know.

And strangely, I'm learning to let "I don't know" be enough.

06 / WHAT I COULDN'T SAY

Maybe one day.
Maybe never.

Maybe one day I'll tell you.

Maybe I won't.

I don't know which possibility scares me more.

Because if I tell you, everything could change.

And if I don't... maybe I'll spend a long time wondering what could have happened.

But I don't want to turn my feelings into promises.

I don't want to make you believe in a future I can't guarantee.

And I don't want to disappear from your life just because I'm afraid of what honesty might do.

So for now, this is where the truth stays.

Here. In a place you will probably never find.

END / NOTHING

Some things are easier
to write to nobody.

I don't know what this becomes.

Maybe nothing.

Maybe just another folder buried somewhere on a computer, forgotten years from now.

Maybe that's okay.

Because not everything real needs to become a relationship.

Not every feeling needs an answer.

Sometimes something can simply matter while it exists.

And you did.

More than you probably ever knew.

— The boy who always said, "Nothing."
